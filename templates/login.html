Your workspace for the symposium

What is this hub?
A shared computing environment for the {{ custom.org.name }}. You work in JupyterLab, with RStudio also available, and Schmidt Observatory System data is already connected. There is nothing to install.
Who can sign in?
Symposium attendees, instructors and organizers, using the event password from the organizers.
How long will my work be kept?
The hub is for the symposium only and will be shut down after the event. Download your notebooks or push them to GitHub to keep them.

AI interactions

To help improve AI-assisted research software tools, this hub logs your interactions with AI models (prompts, model responses, plugin usage patterns and session metadata) through the LiteLLM AI Gateway of the LLMoxie AI platform. These logs are:

  • De-identified. They are not linked to your name.
  • Used for research only. They serve research and product improvement and nothing else.
  • Not shared in identifiable form. They are never shared with third parties in a re-identifiable form.
  • Not used for model training purposes. These requests are routed to Microsoft Foundry, where data is only retained for harmful content screening.
